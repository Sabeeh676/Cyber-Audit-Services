Regulatory Information Systems Audit

Regulatory Information Systems Audit

Regulated organisations must demonstrate that their technology, information security and operational resilience controls are appropriately designed, implemented and regularly reviewed.

Our IS audit services support organisations operating under requirements and expectations established by:

UK Gambling Commission

Certain remote gambling operators must arrange an annual independent security audit against specified controls aligned with ISO/IEC 27001.

Financial Conduct Authority and Prudential Regulation Authority

Regulated financial firms must maintain effective technology governance, cyber resilience, outsourcing controls and operational resilience arrangements.

European Supervisory Authorities

Banks and other EU financial entities must meet DORA requirements covering ICT risk management, resilience testing, incident management, third party risk and independent audit review.

Information Commissioner’s Office and European data protection authorities

Organisations must implement appropriate technical and organisational controls to protect personal information and demonstrate accountability.

Payment card requirements

Organisations processing cardholder information may require independent assurance against PCI DSS requirements.

Why Choose Us?

We provide independent, risk based assessments focused on both regulatory requirements and practical business risks. Our approach includes evidence review, stakeholder interviews, control testing and clear reporting.

Clients receive:

Clearly defined audit scope and objectives

Evidence based findings and risk ratings

Regulatory and control gap analysis

Practical, prioritised recommendations

Management action and remediation plans

Follow up reviews to confirm corrective actions

We help organisations identify control weaknesses, strengthen regulatory readiness and provide management with clear assurance over critical information systems.

Formal regulatory, certification or statutory audits will only be undertaken where the required auditor qualifications, independence and regulatory eligibility criteria are satisfied.
