Regulatory authorities

UK Gambling Commission

Certain remote gambling operators must arrange an annual independent security audit against controls aligned with ISO/IEC 27001.

Financial Conduct Authority and Prudential Regulation Authority

Regulated financial firms must keep effective technology governance, cyber resilience, outsourcing controls and operational resilience arrangements.

European Supervisory Authorities

Banks and other EU financial entities must meet DORA requirements for ICT risk, resilience testing, incident management, third party risk and independent audit review.

Information Commissioner’s Office and European data protection authorities

Organisations must apply appropriate technical and organisational controls for personal information and be able to show accountability.

Payment card requirements

Organisations that process cardholder information may need independent assurance against PCI DSS.
