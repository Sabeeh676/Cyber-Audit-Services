How We Conduct an Audit

We begin by understanding the organisation, its critical systems, regulatory responsibilities and key risks. We then agree the audit scope, evidence requirements and reporting arrangements.

Our auditors review policies, procedures, risk registers, system configurations, access records, change records, security logs and supporting technical evidence. We interview responsible personnel, observe processes and test samples to establish whether controls are properly designed and operating effectively.

Where authorised, our technical specialists can examine application source code, websites, APIs, databases, networks and cloud environments to identify vulnerabilities, insecure configurations, exposed credentials and weaknesses in authentication, encryption or access control.
