How the work is carried out

1

Scope

Every engagement starts with the systems, rules, and outcome written down.

2

Method

Tests follow a stated approach, including OWASP for web applications.

3

Evidence

What was seen is recorded so a finding can be checked again.

4

Clarity

Reports separate severity, impact, and the fix.

5

Discretion

Client systems and incident material stay within the engagement.

6

Handover

Findings and evidence are passed on in a form the next team can use.
