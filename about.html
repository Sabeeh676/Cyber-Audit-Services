About

Testing, review, and response in one place

Cyber Security Audit Services is for organisations that need an outside look at their systems, a check against a named standard, or help after an incident.

What the work covers

VAPT

Web applications against the OWASP Top 10, APIs, networks, AWS and Azure, adversary simulation, and phishing exercises.

Compliance & Audit

PCI DSS, NIST CSF, FIPS 140-2 cryptographic review, and audits of firewalls, segmentation, and secure tunnels.

Forensics

Evidence acquisition, iOS, Android, and SIM forensics, malware analysis, root cause work, and chain of custody.

How an engagement runs

  • Agree the systems, the rules of engagement, and the question the work should answer.
  • Test or review against that scope, and keep notes that can be rechecked.
  • Deliver findings with severity, impact, and a practical next step.
  • Where evidence is involved, keep the handling record with the material.
